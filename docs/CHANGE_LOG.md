# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Add blood glucose and the insulin index, in **mmol/L**, and the four-item diagnostic chain that reads it:
  - `glucose` and `insulin` are new `ModelState` fields. Glucose is the second quantity in the model that is *spent* rather than regulated to a set point: only food puts it back, and the fasting path runs from 5.0 to the 3.5 floor in exactly two game days, slowing down past it.
  - The reference range is **4.0 – 5.5 mmol/L**. Above **8.0** the insulin index climbs twice as steeply, so the higher the sugar the more insulin and the faster it comes down; a meal of anything from 6 to 30 mmol/L is back inside the reference range within half a game day.
  - Food raises glucose by class: **bread +0.7**, **cooked meat and fish +0.5**, **raw meat and fish +0.4**, **plant food +0.4**. Everything edible that is not meat or bread counts as plant food, so living on berries does not avoid the cost.
  - Hypoglycaemia, on the way down: **< 2.0 mining fatigue**, **< 1.7 adds weakness**, **< 1.3 adds magic damage** of `(1.3 - glucose)` every two seconds. Eight game days without food leaves a body at 0.97 mmol/L.
  - The body's own insulin is switched off at the bottom of the reference range, so it can never drive a player hypoglycaemic on its own; injected insulin aspart is not switched off by anything.
- Add `aliment:insulin_injection` (insulin aspart, +10.0 a dose, ceiling 60.0, cleared over one game day). It is the one drug in the mod that makes a body worse on purpose: one dose from a normal 5.0 is a survivable dip to about 2.7, and two are a crisis.
- Add `aliment:glucose_meter`, `aliment:glucose_test_strip`, `aliment:bloodied_test_strip` and `aliment:microneedle`:
  - Right-click with the microneedle to prick a finger, which then bleeds for **15 seconds** (300 ticks); the lancet takes a point of damage.
  - Right-click with a test strip while the finger is still bleeding and it becomes a bloodied one. A strip used after the drop has dried is refused with an action-bar hint, and the drop is spent on the strip it was caught on.
  - Hold the meter in the **main** hand and the bloodied strip in the **off** hand and right-click to print `Blood glucose: 5.1 mmol/L` in chat (`当前血糖：5.1 mmol/L` in `zh_cn`); the strip is used up.
- Add the glucose chain to the village and pillager outpost chests: the insulin injection at **10%**, and the meter, test strips (**5–9** at a time) and microneedle at **35%** each.
- Add the five item sprites, generated by the new `tools/gen_glucose_textures.ps1`.
- Add JEI (Just Enough Items) recipe viewer support for grindstone grinding/crushing recipes.
- Add wheat brewing and beer distillation pipeline:
  - Wheat (`minecraft:wheat`) can now be added into the glass fermentation tank as a fermentable substrate alternative to sugar.
  - Inoculating the tank with brewer's yeast (`aliment:brewer_yeast`) initiates fermentation, producing fermented beer mash.
  - Distilling the fermented wheat mash over a campfire through a condenser pipe into an empty cauldron produces a Beer Cauldron (`aliment:beer_cauldron`).
  - Bottling the beer cauldron with a glass bottle yields Beer (`aliment:beer`, 5% ethanol), providing hydration and mild alcoholic effect when consumed.
  - Added full visual textures and models for beer item, beer cauldron, and fermentation tank liquid states (`wheat` and `beer`).
- Add multi-tier alcohol cauldron distillation pipeline and dynamic alcohol naming:
  - 7% wine and 40% wine can now be poured into a cauldron.
  - Heating an alcohol cauldron over a lit campfire with a condenser pipe above it distills the liquid into a collection cauldron:
    - 7% wine distills through the condenser pipe into 40% wine.
    - 40% wine distills through the condenser pipe into 75% alcohol.
    - 75% alcohol can be poured back into a cauldron and distilled into 98% alcohol.
  - Dynamic naming: Items with ethanol concentration >= 70% (75% and 98%) are named "Alcohol" instead of "Wine".
  - Added dedicated visual models for 7% wine cauldron (rich reddish liquid) and clear distilled alcohol cauldrons (40%, 75%, 98%).
- Add full Korean language localization support (`ko_kr.json`) covering all blocks, items, entities, creative tabs, advancements, tooltips, and JEI recipes.
- Add a stimulant sleep restriction: a player whose ephedrine concentration exceeds 0.5 can no longer fall asleep, and attempting to use a bed shows "You cannot sleep while stimulated".
  - The threshold lives in the Scala model (`EPHEDRINE_SLEEP_BLOCK_THRESHOLD`), is re-exported through `AlimentModelBridge`, and is mirrored in `AlimentData`.
  - The restriction is enforced by `PlayerSleepMixin`, which injects into `Player.startSleepInBed` and returns a vanilla `BedSleepingProblem` carrying the message, so the refusal reuses vanilla's own display path (the same one that reports an occupied bed) with no extra HUD code.
  - Creative players stay frozen and are unaffected; sleep becomes possible again as the ephedrine is metabolised (fully cleared within one in-game day).

### Changed
- A bloodied test strip now carries **the reading it took**, as a data component in mmol/L, and the meter reports that instead of the player's current blood glucose. A sample that kept changing after it was taken would say nothing about the moment it was taken; the stored value also survives the strip being stored, dropped or handed to another player. A strip with no sample on it falls back to the body's current glucose.
- Mark the fermentation tank's and the condenser pipe's glass texture as `"force_translucent": true` in all nineteen of their models. In 26.3 that texture-level flag - `{"force_translucent": true, "sprite": "..."}` - is how a sprite opts into translucent rendering: vanilla uses it 163 times and **no** vanilla model uses a model-level `render_type` at all, so the flag is the mechanism, not `render_type`.
- Redraw the fermentation tank and condenser pipe as **copper-framed opaque frosted glass vessels**, with a vertical **sight glass** down the tank's panel so the fill level can still be read. Earlier passes left the middle of both textures fully transparent, which in game read as a wireframe cage you could see the world through rather than a container; the panel is now opaque, so the block has a solid silhouette. The sight glass runs the full height of the panel because the three fill levels sit far apart - level 1 tops out near texture row 11.5, level 2 at 6.4 and level 3 at 1.3 - and a short window would not show a nearly empty tank at all. Two details: the **sight glass's transparent pixels carry the panel's colour rather than black**, because Minecraft averages RGBA per texel when it builds mipmaps and `(0,0,0,0)` drags that average towards black, ringing the window with a dark edge; and the **frame is sized in world units, not texels**, because a block model stretches whatever rectangle of the texture it is handed across the whole face - the tank's faces are near square and take a symmetric 3-texel rim (about 2.25 world units), while the condenser pipe is a 6-unit tube whose side faces are 6 wide by 16 tall, squeezing the texture 16 -> 6 horizontally and leaving it 1:1 vertically, so its rim is 4 texels across and 2 down (about 1.5 against 2.0 world units). Generated by `tools/gen_glass_textures.ps1`.
- Redraw potion and beer textures adhering to authentic vanilla Minecraft item style:
  - Coptis Potion (`aliment:coptis_potion`): vibrant golden-yellow herbal potion based on vanilla potion bottle and grayscale overlay.
  - Phellodendron Potion (`aliment:phellodendron_potion`): deep amber-gold herbal potion based on vanilla potion bottle and grayscale overlay.
  - Licorice Potion (`aliment:licorice_potion`): warm caramel-honey brown herbal potion based on vanilla potion bottle and grayscale overlay.
  - Beer (`aliment:beer`): vanilla bottle shape filled with golden-amber beer and frothy creamy foam at the neck.
- Add hand-swing use animations, client prediction, and insertion particles (`WHITE_SMOKE`, `COMPOSTER`, `FALLING_HONEY`, `SPLASH`) when placing items into the glass fermentation tank and scooping liquid from cauldrons.
- Consolidate all documentation into the `docs/` directory:
  - Moved `SPOILER.md`, `PHYSIOLOGY.md`, `TECHNICAL.md`, `CHANGE_LOG.md`, and `MODRINTH.md` (with their `_zh` counterparts) from the repository root into `docs/`.
  - `README.md` and `README_zh.md` stay at the root so GitHub renders them as the repository landing page.
  - Updated every cross-reference in `README.md`, `README_zh.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `docs/MODRINTH.md`, `docs/SPOILER.md`, and `docs/SPOILER_zh.md`; all documentation links were re-verified to resolve.
